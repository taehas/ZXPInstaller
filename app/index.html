    About

    ZXP Manager

    A modern fork of ZXPInstaller that installs, audits and cleanly removes Adobe CEP and UXP extensions.

    Heads up: this fork is vibe-coded. It was built quickly with an AI coding assistant and is tested by use, not by a full test suite. It never hard-deletes (removals go to the Trash), but read the confirmation dialogs and keep backups of anything you can't replace.

    Original ZXPInstaller by @maxoos, maintained by @cameronmcefee and ELEMENTS Media Storage. This fork keeps the same GPL-2.0 license.
